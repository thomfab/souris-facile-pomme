# What's new

The section of a version becomes the notes of its GitHub release.

## 0.5

- Letting go of the click after snapping a window to the grid now finishes
  the move or resize. The window stays where it snapped, and the keys do
  nothing until you release them, so the window can't drift off afterwards.

## 0.4

- **Free by default, snap on demand.** Moving and resizing are free again,
  with edges only snapping to screen edges and nearby windows. Press and hold
  the click (a normal trackpad click) while holding the keys to snap to the
  grid; the grid lines and the dimming only show then.
- **A grid on every display.** No more switch to turn the grid on: each
  display has one, and a display seen for the first time gets a size that
  suits it, from 2 × 2 on a 14″ laptop to 8 × 4 on an ultrawide.
- The "Ultrawide, fine" preset is now 8 × 4, and there's a new "Portrait"
  preset (2 × 3).

## 0.3

- **Window sizes remembered per display.** Each display remembers where each
  app's windows last were. A window sent or dragged to a display gets that
  size and place back, so plugging into your office monitor puts things where
  they were last time.
- **A new settings window,** with a sidebar: General, Move, Resize, Snapping
  and Send to Display, plus a page for each display with its own grid and
  remembered window sizes, and one for unplugged displays.

## 0.2

- **Send a window to another display** while holding the move keys: swipe two
  fingers on a trackpad or Magic Mouse toward a display, turn the mouse wheel
  to go to the next one, or press an arrow key. The window keeps its place
  and size relative to the screen, and the pointer goes with it.
- **A grid size for each display,** remembered when the display is unplugged
  and used again when it's plugged back in.

## 0.1

- First release. Hold Control + Option and move the mouse to move the window
  under the pointer; add Command to resize it from the nearest corner.
- Window edges snap to screen edges and nearby windows, or to a grid.
- A menu bar icon with About, an on/off switch and the settings, and launch at
  login.
